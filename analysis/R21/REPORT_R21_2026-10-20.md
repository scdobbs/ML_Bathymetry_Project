## B1. Target filtering (Text S3.1)

**Ready to paste.** Targets were computed on 256 × 256-pixel windows of the AUV grid laid on a lattice of stride 128 pixels; the scored tile is the central 128 × 128 pixels of its window, so tiles do not overlap. In each window the AUV elevations minus δ (a scalar: the median of AUV minus upsampled ship over the window's joint-valid pixels, when at least 64 such pixels exist) were set to zero outside the joint-valid mask, a least-squares plane fitted over the joint-valid pixels was removed (invalid pixels set to zero), and a separable two-dimensional Tukey taper with α = 0.25 was applied. The band-pass filter is a radial mask in the two-dimensional Fourier domain with raised-cosine edges in log2 frequency: unit gain between f_lr × 2^0.25 and f_hi × 2^−0.25, each edge ramping over ±0.25 octave (half-cosine, total width 0.5 octave), zero at DC, with f_lr = AUV pixel / lr_native and f_hi = min(k f_lr, 0.45) cycles per pixel. The filtered field is the real part of the inverse FFT, cropped to the central 128 × 128 pixels and re-masked to joint-valid. A_fine is the root mean square of this field over the tile's joint-valid pixels; no mean is removed at this step (the plane was removed before filtering and the filter has zero DC gain), and the only count rule is that a tile with no joint-valid pixel is NaN. T_fine is the same statistic on the field after subtracting the bicubic upsampled ship grid. A window was admitted if at least 50 % of its pixels were joint-valid, at least 50 % AUV-valid, its AUV standard deviation was at least 0.05 m and it covered at least two ship cells; a tile entered the frozen table tiles_v1.1 if at least 50 % of its central 128 × 128 pixels were joint-valid, and 32-pixel blocks were kept if at least 75 % joint-valid.

**Sources.** `analysis/R04/scripts/targets_v1.py:20-24` (constants), `:26-41` (mask), `:48` (Tukey), `:50-65` (plane, filter, crop), `:73-80` (RMS), `:82-93` (blocks); tag `phase0-freeze` (commit 866320b); `analysis/R04/scripts/build_tiles_targets.py:29-37, 56, 68`; `analysis/R04/scripts/r04_common.py:156-158` (band edges); `preprocessing/extract_clean_patches.py:55, 59-62, 68-76, 131-146` (δ, upsampling, admission rules); `analysis/R05/scripts/build_tiles_v1_1.py:8` and `r05_common.py:9` (`TILE_FLOOR = 0.5`).

## B2. Spectral extrapolation (Text S4.1)

**Ready to paste.** The spectral-extrapolation predictor ŝ_fine was computed on the native ship-grid window of each tile (n = max(2, ceil(window / ratio)) cells on a side, ratio = ship cell / AUV pixel; invalid cells mean-filled; at least 16 valid cells and 25 % valid). A least-squares plane was removed over the valid cells, invalid cells set to zero, a two-dimensional Hann taper normalized by its RMS applied, and the periodogram |FFT|² / (H W) averaged radially in max(4, min(H, W) / 2) equal-width bins on [0, 0.5] cycles per cell (a single periodogram, no Welch averaging). A power law P(f) = A f^−β was fitted by unweighted least squares in log10–log10 over the bins with at least eight coefficients, 0 < f ≤ 0.4 cycles per cell and positive power (at least three bins, else NaN), and β was clamped to [1, 5]. The predicted fine-band RMS is ŝ_fine = sqrt(2π A W_n ∫_1^k f^(1−β) df) with W_n = H W, evaluated as (k^(2−β) − 1) / (2 − β) (or ln k when β = 2), that is the integral of the fitted power over the annulus f ∈ [1, k].

**Sources.** `analysis/R05/scripts/features_v1_1.py:79-106, 146-158` (frozen, tag `phase0-freeze`; identical code in `analysis/R04/scripts/features_v1.py:77-102` and the original `analysis/R03/scripts/features_context.py:1-11, 40-54`); the 64-cell version `shat_fine_W64` in `analysis/R08/scripts/wide_window_r08.py:27-43`.

**To resolve before pasting.** The fit uses f in cycles per ship cell, where Nyquist is 0.5, but the integration band is [1, k] in the same variable, so it lies entirely above the fitted range; the R03 docstring calls this band "[f_lr, f_hi] = [1, k] cyc/cell". The SI should describe the band exactly as coded or explain the intended scaling.

## B3. GBT (Text S4.2)

**Ready to paste.** GBT_ship is a scikit-learn `HistGradientBoostingRegressor` (scikit-learn 1.7.2, recorded in the R09 artifact record and confirmed in the environment) fitted on 72 ship-only features and predicting log10(A_fine + 10^−9) at the tile scale; predictions are back-transformed as 10^pred. The features are, at two window sizes (128 and 224 AUV pixels): from the native ship window, the number of cells per side, the valid fraction, the mean (μ_lr) and standard deviation (s_lr, floored at 0.5 m) of the valid cells, the standard deviation after plane removal, the mean and 90th percentile of the slope magnitude, the standard deviation of the five-point Laplacian curvature, and the spectral set β, log A, fit R², clamped β, ŝ_fine, the noise excess at f ∈ [0.4, 0.5] and the spectral anisotropy with the number of fitted bins; on the ×8 cubic upsampling of the mean-filled window, the band-passed RMS C in [1/16, 1/8] cycles per pixel, its curvature RMS, its anisotropy and the mean slope after Gaussian smoothing; the seven contract-product statistics (median and 90th percentile of ship_rsd, median ship_sd, median log10 ship_count, mean ship_xtrack_frac, mean ship_beam_angle, median ship_rsd / ship_sd; NaN for pairs whose products are not usable); on the AUV-grid lattice of the window, the same roughness set and slope computed on the bicubic of the area-averaged ship grid, the mean distance to the nearest invalid ship pixel and the ship-valid fraction; and the metadata s_lr of the 256-pixel parent window, depth (μ_lr at 128), lr_native, k, AUV pixel and log10(|depth| / lr_native). GBT_noship drops the 14 contract-product features (58 features). The configuration was chosen by nested leave-one-unit-out search over 48 configurations (max_depth ∈ {2, 3, 4, 6} × trees ∈ {100, 300, 600} × learning rate ∈ {0.03, 0.1} × min_samples_leaf ∈ {20, 100}; early stopping off): for each outer held-out unit and each configuration, an inner leave-one-unit-out over the remaining units scored each inner unit by the median over its eligible pairs of the within-pair Spearman ρ, the configuration with the highest median inner-unit score was selected (ties to the lowest index), and the outer model was fitted on all other units. Training rows were capped at 200 per unit by one random draw per unit (seed 20261001, reused in every fold); held-out units are scored on all their rows. The all-16-unit model used for the blind tests (R09) selected configuration 18: max_depth 3, 300 trees, learning rate 0.1, min_samples_leaf 20 (inner criterion 0.556; 2,788 training rows of 23,438). The random state of every model and of the row cap is 20261001.

**Sources.** `analysis/R05/scripts/bar_v1_1.py:24, 29-34, 39, 42-49, 58-64, 68-97, 114-135` (frozen, `phase0-freeze`; identical to `R04/scripts/bar_v1.py` except the inner criterion, `bar_v1.py:7`); `analysis/R05/scripts/features_v1_1.py:30, 59-77, 110, 143-215`; `analysis/R04/scripts/build_features.py:53-55`; `analysis/R09/scripts/refit_gbt16_r09.py:16-33`; `A/R09/artifacts/gbt_ship_all16_record.json` (`cfg_idx 18`, parameters, `sklearn_version 1.7.2`, `n_features 72`, `target "log10(A_fine + 1e-9), tile scale"`, `seed 20261001`, `cap 200`). ## B4. CNNs (Text S4.3)

**Ready to paste.** The networks share a U-Net body with base width 32 and channel multipliers (1, 2, 4, 4): four encoder levels of one residual block each (3 × 3 reflect-padded convolutions, per-pixel LayerNorm, SiLU, a 1 × 1 skip) joined by stride-2 3 × 3 convolutions, and three decoder levels of nearest ×2 upsampling, 3 × 3 convolution, skip concatenation and a block, followed by LayerNorm and SiLU. Every block applies FiLM after its first convolution: a two-layer MLP (4 → c → 2c, zero-initialized) maps the four-vector [log2 lr_native, log2 AUV pixel, log2 s_lr, log2 |μ_lr|] to a scale and shift, x (1 + g) + b. The head crops the decoder output to the central 128 pixels (V3 keeps the full window), average-pools to 4 × 4, and applies 1 × 1 convolution, SiLU and a zero-initialized 1 × 1 convolution to one channel, predicting log10 A_fine per 32-pixel block. V2 adds a product branch (eight inputs: four product rasters and their masks; three levels of 16, 32, 64 channels with FiLM) concatenated before the head. Parameter counts: V0, V1 and V3 1,914,081; V2 2,055,809 (branch 133,408). Inputs are the ship side only: the bicubic upsampling of the area-averaged ship grid (invalid cells filled with the window mean), standardized per window as (x − μ_lr) / s_lr with s_lr floored at 0.5 m, plus the nearest-upsampled ship validity; no constants and no AUV statistic enter (verified by a leak test in which the AUV grid, its mask and the joint mask were replaced by noise and the inputs were byte-identical). V2 products enter as log10 max(ship_rsd, 1 mm), log10 ship_count, ship_xtrack_frac and ship_beam_angle / 90 with masks, and are dropped together with probability 0.3 during training. The target is log10 max(A_fine, 10^−4) per kept block; the tile prediction is the root of the mean over the 16 blocks of 10^(2 pred). V0 minimizes the kept-block-weighted mean squared error; V1, V2 and V3 minimize the group-centered error, in which the kept-block means of prediction and target are subtracted within each group of eight windows drawn from one pair. Training: AdamW, learning rate 2 × 10^−4 with cosine annealing to 10^−6 over 8,000 steps, weight decay 10^−4, gradient clipping at 1.0, bf16 autocast, batches of four groups of eight windows (32 windows) sampled unit-uniform then pair-uniform, augmentation by the eight square symmetries drawn per window. There is no early stopping and no validation split: the final checkpoint at step 8,000 is scored on the held-out unit (16-fold leave-one-unit-out, plus an "ALL" model that predicts EW0207). Seeds: 20261002 for all variants, with 20261003 and 20261004 added for V1 (and for P-V1, P-V2 and V2 in R07); torch and NumPy are seeded, cuDNN is deterministic, the sampler uses the seed and the augmentation the seed + 1. Pretraining (P-V1) used the same body with a 1 × 1 reconstruction head at full resolution and a spread head of the block-head form, trained for 60,000 steps at batch 64 (3,840,000 windows, each presented once) on the ship-only window cache with 30 % of the valid ship cells masked uniformly at random (masked cells enter as missing); the loss is the unweighted sum of the mean squared reconstruction error on masked-cell pixels against the unmasked normalized bicubic and the mean squared error of standardized log10 ship_rsd over covered blocks (P-V2 zeroes the rsd channel); AdamW with the same learning rate, weight decay, cosine schedule, clipping, bf16 and square-symmetry augmentation; seed 20261007; a validation set of 16,384 windows from 13 held-out cruises served only for curves. Fine-tuning initializes the body from the pretrained checkpoint, trains a fresh zero-initialized head and otherwise follows the 8,000-step recipe. Hardware: P-V1 and P-V2 pretraining ran on NVIDIA A100-SXM4-40GB (9.18 h and 12.41 h); the 100 R06 trainings ran on A100-SXM4-40GB (64), A100-SXM4-80GB (11), L40S (16) and H100 80GB (9); the 136 R07 fine-tunings on A100-40GB (63), A100-80GB (61) and H100 (12).

**Sources.** `analysis/R06/scripts/r06_model.py:15-22, 26-33, 35-54, 56-69, 76-87`; `analysis/R06/scripts/build_inputs_r06.py:1-14, 48-55, 96, 114-120`; `analysis/R06/scripts/train_r06.py:8-9, 51-63, 66-79, 81-93, 99-100, 108-125, 153`; `analysis/R06/scripts/r06_common.py:44, 51-56`; `analysis/R07/scripts/pretrain_r07.py:76-79, 157`, `r07_pretext_model.py:16-33`, `r07_windows.py:101-106`, `r07_common.py:48-62`, `train_r07.py:90-99`; parameter counts from every `train_log.json` under `A/R06/models/` and `A/R07/models/` and the R06 report §2 item 3 and §3 line 71, R07 report lines 66, 188, 242; GPUs from the per-run `train_log.json` records and the R06 report lines 105-114, R07 report lines 132, 261, 454.
