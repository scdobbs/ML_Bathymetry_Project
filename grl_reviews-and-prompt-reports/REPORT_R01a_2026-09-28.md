**Does any HR-derived quantity reach the network's inputs at evaluation? YES. CONFIRMED.**
The only input channel is (ship grid − AUV patch mean) / AUV patch std, with both statistics computed
from the AUV grid over the tile's valid pixels at preprocessing (`P:305–314`) and read unchanged at
evaluation (`H:200`). The target is (AUV − AUV mean) / AUV std, so it has mean 0 and std 1 on every patch by
construction. Three consequences follow from the code alone:

1. The vertical ship-minus-AUV offset is visible in the input as its spatial mean (A5 shows this offset is
   0.5 to 1.7 normalized units at the median and up to 117). A predictor that subtracts the mean of its
   input removes the offset using AUV information, not seafloor information.
2. The residual variance the network must explain is a function of the normalized input alone
   (1 + var(input) − 2 cov(input, target), with the target's variance fixed at 1), so part of the per-patch
   "total error" magnitude is encoded in the input's amplitude. σ can learn that without reading seafloor.
3. On a low-relief tile, a meter-scale offset becomes an input of order 10 to 100 (A4: 3,275 native patches
   above 10), which then meets zero padding at the tile border and the HR-mean fill at coverage edges.

The `scale` vector carries only the two grid spacings and is not a leak. Nothing in the code reads HR at
inference time other than through these stored normalized arrays.

**Per §0.1(ii) and B1, this is an escalation. Phase B stopped here; B2 to B5 and Phase C are HELD.**

## 7. Candidate fixes for a retrain (R02), each tied to R01a evidence (listed, not ranked)

- Normalize inputs and targets with **LR-derived** statistics only (B1 table, P:305–314); the LR mean and std
  over the tile's LR-valid cells are available at inference without AUV data.
- Fill invalid LR cells with the **LR** level, not the HR mean (P:312), to remove the offset step at coverage
  edges.
- Predict in meters, or normalize by a robust LR scale with a floor, so that low-relief tiles do not produce
  inputs above 10 (A4).
- Reflect or replicate padding, or train on larger tiles and evaluate with a rim (B1 padding row; theoretical
  RF 119 px).
- Treat the per-patch vertical offset as an explicit nuisance (estimate it from LR-only statistics or drop
  it from the target) rather than letting the network absorb it (A5).
- Exclude or down-weight EW0207 in training and report it separately (A7).
