## 3. Challenge clause (§0) — wrong or underspecified instructions, with evidence, and the executor's calls

1. **§2 "≥ 95 % of its joint-valid px".** The tile footprint is the 128 × 128-px tile of tiles_v1.1 (`tr0`, `tc0`); every tile has ≥ 50 % joint-valid px by the R05 rule, so the fraction is always defined. Pixels outside the mosaic count as invalid through a one-px invalid pad before the distance transform; the mosaic itself is NaN wherever no accepted window covers it, which the joint mask already encodes as invalid.
2. **§2 D₁ from manifest v2.1.** lr_native is read from the manifest row and asserted equal to the tiles_v1.1 value; hr_res comes from the mosaic transform and is asserted equal to the tiles_v1.1 value. D₁ = ⌈lr_native / hr_res⌉ (5–66 px).
3. **§2 eligibility "applied to the interior subset".** Computed per predictor with `eval_v1_1.eligible_pairs` on each subset (finite target and finite prediction, ≥ 10 tiles), which is the frozen function; the drop ledger uses the target-only version (the two coincide for every fitted predictor; ref_rsd128 and the within-site column additionally lose their NaN pairs, as in R05 / R08).
4. **§4 "difference of medians (as `eval_v1_1.compare` gives it)".** `compare` keeps the units scored on both sides, so a Δ of medians is over the common units (9 on the ten at D₁, because lu14 has no interior tile), while the median columns use every scored unit of that subset (n shown in each cell). The paired median uses the same common units and the same resamples.
5. **§3 round-1 V2.** R06's V2 (seed 20261002) and R07's `V2_s20261002` are the same predictions (asserted bit-identical); it appears once, as `V2_s20261002`. On EW0207 V1 exists only at seed 20261002 (R06), so no V1 seed-averaged EW0207 row.
6. **§1 appendix parsing.** The 39 rows were parsed from the directive's table by cell; empty criterion cells are stored as empty strings; `relief` is "weak" on P19 and P27 as written. Nothing was edited.
7. **§4 reading guide.** Applied to the paired median Δ and its CI, as written; the differences of medians on the same comparisons (−0.106 for ref_s_lr, −0.103 for GBT_ship) exceed −0.10 in magnitude but carry lu14's exit from the unit set, which is why the guide names the paired statistic.
8. **Working tree.** As in R07 / R08: uncommitted June 2026 edits to `models/`, `training/`, `preprocessing/data_utils.py`, `CLAUDE.md`, `METHOD.md` remain; no R08b script imports those modules (only the R04–R08 directive code and `scipy`). COULD NOT CHECK beyond that.
