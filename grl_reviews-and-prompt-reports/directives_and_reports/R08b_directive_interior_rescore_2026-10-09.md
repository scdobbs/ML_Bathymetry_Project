## 2. Interior tiles

**Invalid px:** every px of a pair's mosaic that is not joint-valid in `joint_v1.tif`. Px outside the mosaic also count as
invalid (pad with invalid before the distance transform).

**Distance:** Euclidean distance transform, in HR px, from each px to the nearest invalid px
(`scipy.ndimage.distance_transform_edt` on the valid mask).

**Interior distance per pair:** D₁ = ⌈lr_native / hr_res⌉ px (one lr_native, the band's longest wavelength), from
manifest v2.1. Also D₂ = 2 × D₁ as a sensitivity.

**Interior tile (fixed now):** a `tiles_v1.1` tile is interior at level Dᵢ if **≥ 95 % of its joint-valid px** have
distance ≥ Dᵢ.

**Eligibility:** unchanged from `eval_v1.1` (≥ 10 tiles per pair with finite target and prediction), applied to the
interior subset. List every pair and unit that drops out at D₁ and at D₂.

**Report per pair and per unit:** D₁ (px and m), tiles total, interior tiles at D₁ and D₂, and the fraction kept.

**Check:** report the median of tile A_fine on interior tiles against all tiles, per pair and per unit. If the edge
inflation is what R08 suggests, interior medians are lower, most of all on the six.
