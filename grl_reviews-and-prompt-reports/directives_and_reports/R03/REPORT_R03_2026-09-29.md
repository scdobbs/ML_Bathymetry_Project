## 3. §2 — Decomposition of σ_m's skill (CONFIRMED; job 45924930, `s2/`)

σ_m = σ_norm × s_lr holds exactly per tile (R02's tile statistic is the mean of per-pixel σ over scored pixels, and
s_lr is constant on a tile), so log σ_m = log σ_norm + log s_lr. Same metric machinery as R02 T33 (`r03_metrics.py`).
Partial: per-unit OLS of log σ_norm and of log T on log s_lr; Pearson r of the residuals, pooled; bootstrap over units.

**Feature extractor** (`scripts/features_context.py`, 6,737 tiles = R02's 200-per-pair subsample rule on all 33 pairs,
EW0207 included but scored separately): ship windows cut from the harmonized LR raster centred on the tile at (a) the
tile extent, (b) tile + 48 HR px, (c) 32 × 32 and (d) 64 × 64 ship cells; ship validity reprojected onto the LR grid
(first attempt indexed `lr_valid.tif`, which lives on the HR grid, and lost 57% of windows — fixed, job 45936095 →
45952843). R02's roughness-3 / slope features are computed at every context on the bicubic interpolant of the ship
window at a fixed ×8 factor with the same bands in cycles per ship cell; at (a) they agree with the exact R02
HR-grid computation to log-correlation 0.953 (`*_a_hrgrid` columns hold the exact values and are what the "R02" rows
use). §4.2 spectral fit on the native window: plane removed, Hann taper, radial PSD, log–log fit over [f_min, 0.4]
cyc/cell (f_min = first bin with ≥ 8 coefficients), β clamped to [1, 5], A f^−β integrated over the annulus
[1, k] cyc/cell → ŝ_fine (m); noise indicator = log₁₀(measured / fitted power in [0.4, 0.5] cyc/cell); anisotropy =
√(eigen-ratio) of the power-weighted frequency covariance. **Feature leak test: 100/100 tiles byte-identical with HR
replaced by noise** (`s4/features_all_leak.json`). GBT = task5 protocol (150 trees, lr 0.06, depth 3), nested LOUO,
**Cal DIG capped to 200 rows in every training set** (D2; evaluated on all its 3,574 tiles).

**Availability caveat (bears on every (a)/(b) row).** A tile holds 4–9 ship cells on NA080, FK181031, TN299, TN399,
EW0207 and NA090, so the spectral features (and therefore the expanded bank) are undefined at contexts (a) and (b)
there: on the 7 native units the (a) rows cover 2 units / 386 tiles and the (b) rows 4 units / 762 tiles; on all 15
units, 10 and 12 units. Contexts (c) and (d) cover every unit (6,521 / 1,547 tiles). For k = 2 pairs (Cal DIG, 52
cells per tile) contexts (c) and (d) are smaller than the tile. Median cells per unit: a = 4–18 (Cal DIG 52), b = 6–25
