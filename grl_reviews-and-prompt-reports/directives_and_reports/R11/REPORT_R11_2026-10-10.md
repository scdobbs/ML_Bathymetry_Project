For pairs whose HR spacing exceeds 1 m (AT42-06 5 m, KM0923 / EW9801 / tag_m127 / AR26 about 2 m) the 256-px window is wider than 256 m, so their cells across stay above the sealed values; the sealed pairs' HR spacing is 1.0–1.04 m.
