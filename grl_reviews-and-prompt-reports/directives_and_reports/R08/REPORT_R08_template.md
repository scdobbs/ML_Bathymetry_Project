## 2. Challenge clause (§0) — wrong or underspecified instructions, with evidence, and the executor's calls

1. **§1 fold assignment.** "Assign blocks to 5 folds with `default_rng(20261009)`" does not say how. Implemented per pair: the sorted unique 4 × 4-tile blocks are permuted with a fresh `default_rng(20261009)` and dealt round-robin into 5 folds (balanced; every fold non-empty when ≥ 5 blocks). `HistGradientBoostingRegressor` is given `early_stopping=False` explicitly (sklearn's default `'auto'` would enable it above 10,000 rows; no training set exceeds 6,264 rows, asserted in code), and the target is log₁₀(A_fine + 10⁻⁹) as in the bar. "All features available for that pair" = the bar's 72 columns minus those all-NaN for the pair (recorded per pair in `feasibility.json`).
2. **§1 feasibility.** "≥ 5 spatial blocks with at least one test tile each" is satisfied by ≥ 5 blocks under the round-robin dealing (every block holds ≥ 1 tile). The 40-training-tile floor after the buffer is the binding constraint (HOLD H1).
3. **§2 "the frozen R05 GBT_ship models … if not persisted, refit".** R05 persisted predictions and chosen configurations, not models. Refit = the bar's outer fit (`cap_mask` rows of the other 15 units, float32 features, the R05 configuration per unit, `random_state` 20261001); max |difference| 4.4 × 10⁻¹⁶ m (`tables/refit_check.md`).
4. **§2 group mapping.** 15 of the 72 columns are not named in the directive's table; they are assigned by what they measure and marked † in `tables/importance_groups.md` (s_lr_224, slope_128/224, beta_clamped, n_fit_bins, mu_lr, n_cells, valid_frac, curv_hr). "Other" is empty. valid_frac_128/224 (the native ship window's valid fraction) sits in edge-and-validity with dist_edge and frac_valid; **the drop-group refit therefore removes six columns, not the two the directive names**. If Steve wants the literal two-column drop, the refit is a 5-minute CPU rerun.
5. **§2 permutation stream.** One `default_rng(20261009)` stream, consumed serially in the order units (units-v1 order) → groups (table order) → 30 permutations → pairs (sorted), one permutation per pair applied to all of the group's columns. Unit ρ is `eval_v1_1.score_one` on the unit's rows with the R05 eligible-pair set; the unpermuted unit ρ equals R05's to 10⁻¹² (asserted).
6. **§3.2 directional concentration.** Computed on the 128-px tiles of tiles_v1.1 (NaN → 0, no extra taper beyond the Tukey taper already in `F_hr`), power summed over tiles, annulus [f_lr, f_hi] of the pair, 18 sectors of 10° in orientation mod 180°. 0.056 = 1 / 18 (the directive's "10 % expected for isotropy" is read as 10° / 180°).
7. **§3.3 subwindow rule.** "The largest square inside the valid area, centred on its centroid" is read with a tolerance: the largest square (≤ 2 km, ≥ 1 km) centred on the centroid of the joint-valid area whose joint-valid fraction is ≥ 0.9; the centre moves to the nearest valid px if the centroid is invalid (never needed). 8 of 39 pairs never reach 0.9 (narrow swaths or sparse coverage) and show the best square found, listed with their valid fraction in `tables/ledger.md`. Pairs ≤ 2.5 km across are shown whole (none: every pair's mosaic exceeds 2.5 km). The hillshade uses a vertical exaggeration where the window's 95th-percentile slope is below 5° (raised so that it reads 20°; factor on the set-B title), because the millimetre units are otherwise featureless.
8. **§4 "unit medians".** Median over the unit's tiles (all pairs pooled), as in the R07 per-unit table; the per-pair values are in `tables/level_per_pair.md`. The band width uses log₂ k as instructed; the effective width log₂(f_hi / f_lr) equals it on every development pair (no clipping at 0.45).
9. **§5 conditioning variable.** "log s_lr" = log₁₀ of the tile's `s_lr` (tiles_v1.1, the R02 256-px statistic = `ref_s_lr`), not s_lr_128. Partial Spearman = Pearson correlation of the residuals of the two rank vectors regressed on the rank of log s_lr. EX1402L2 (vu02) has no xtrack feature (R05 override): its xtrack cells are NaN and those medians are over 11 units.
10. **§6 "the frozen 128-px rows (s_lr, C)".** s_lr_128 is the bar's `ref_shipstd128` (asserted equal), not `ref_s_lr` (256 px); C_128 is the ship-window ×8-bicubic C of `native_features`, the same definition as C_W64, whereas the bar's `ref_C` is `C_hr_128` (the HR-grid bicubic). Both are reported. The 128-px ŝ_fine / β rows are undefined on 14 units (HOLD H2).
11. **§6 window placement.** Tile centre from the tiles_v1.1 bounds (centre of the 128-px tile) in the tile CRS, which equals the context CRS on all 39 pairs; `rasterio.transform.rowcol` then `round(r − 32)`, the `ship_window` convention of `features_v1`. 50 of 26,154 tiles have < 50 % valid cells (lu05 19, lu15 21, lu16 9, vu02 1) and are NaN.
12. **Working tree.** As in R07 §2 item 11, the repository carries uncommitted June 2026 edits to `models/`, `training/`, `preprocessing/data_utils.py`, `CLAUDE.md` and `METHOD.md`. The only repository module these scripts import outside `directives_and_reports/` is `preprocessing/colocated_utils.resolve_pair_paths` (not in the diff). COULD NOT CHECK beyond that.
