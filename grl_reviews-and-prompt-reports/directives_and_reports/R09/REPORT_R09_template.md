## 2. Challenge clause (§0) — wrong or underspecified instructions, with evidence, and the executor's calls

1. **Executor's defect, found after the jobs ran.** The first `os.scandir` wrapper of the R09 guard returned a plain generator, which Python's `glob` cannot use as a context manager; `freeze_r09.py` (the only R09 script that calls `glob`) failed, and the wrapper was replaced by an object that is both an iterator and a context manager (same filtering). The three jobs and the pre-registration render ran under the earlier wrapper, whose blocking behaviour is identical (702 / 702 probes in every provenance record); `freeze_record.*` and the OAK `scripts_final/` carry the corrected file. Ten notes follow.
2. **§0.2 guard "any path of a confirmatory or lockbox pair raises, including manifest-resolved paths".** The inherited guard (R04 + R07) keys on the full pair id and on the confirmatory directory; the self-test showed that the lockbox pairs' raw paths (`raw_lr/AT42-03`, `raw_lr/TN159`, `staging_phase1/MGDS_32007`, …) carry only a cruise or MGDS token and were not blocked. The R09 guard therefore blocks every path containing a sealed pair's id, cruise part, MGDS part or any manifest path string of its row (37 tokens), and wraps `io.open`, `os.listdir / scandir / stat / lstat / walk`, `glob` and `pathlib.Path.open`, filtering sealed names out of listings. Checked that no path of the 38 development pairs or EW0207 is blocked. This also seals the lockbox cruises' raw swath data, which no development pair shares.
3. **§B2.2 "configuration chosen by the frozen inner criterion over all 16 units".** That rule is not the one R05 used for its EW0207 model (mode of the 16 per-fold choices). Implemented as written (inner leave-one-unit-out over the 16 units for each of the 48 grid points, `bar_v1_1._score_unit`, median over units, ties to the lowest index); the chosen configuration (cfg 18) differs from R05's (cfg 16), hence HOLD H1. The refit uses the R05 data path exactly (`assemble`, `cap_mask`, float32 features, log₁₀ target, seed 20261001).
4. **§A2 "the primary units scored both ways"** = the nine of the ten with interior tiles (lu14 has none), so the validation rule is evaluated on 9 units; lu14 is shown on all tiles and on A_fine_int (§5.4) and is in no median, per ruling §0.3.
5. **§A1 threshold.** Tiles with fewer than 2,048 interior px get NaN (1,045 of 26,154 at D₁; 5,355 at D₁₂₅); eligibility on the A_fine_int views is `eval_v1_1.eligible_pairs` per predictor (≥ 10 tiles with finite target and prediction); no development pair drops at D₁.
6. **§A3 "repeat A1–A2".** D₁₂₅ = ⌈125 / hr_res⌉ with the pair's own hr_res (63 px on 2-m grids, 125–126 px on 1-m grids; the target band is unchanged); both the interior-tile view and the A_fine_int view are scored for all three candidates, and the paired differences are taken against all tiles (the directive asks for survival and scores, not a validation rule, at D₁₂₅).
7. **§B1 "scored by het_production_v1".** The record shows the scoring model was `het_production_v1_repro (all-native)` (the §C reproduction, labelled het_retrain_nonrepro in REPORT_55), since the production weights were never persisted; the pre-registration's history label says so rather than "het_production_v1".
8. **§B5 expectation range.** Recomputed from R08b's per-unit interior-tile ρ for s_lr over the nine primary units with interior tiles: −0.09 to 0.63, median 0.39, equal to the directive's figures (CONFIRMED); lu14's all-tile value (0.81) lies above the range and is noted in the pre-registration as excluded.
9. **§B "completing the bracketed fields".** The directive text carries no literal brackets; the fields completed are the fallback status, the lockbox history, the artifact paths and hashes, the expectation range and the frozen code hashes (`scores/prereg_render.json` lists every value). The B3–B6 text is otherwise verbatim.
10. **§B2.1 s_lr "computed from the sealed pairs' LR only".** Recorded as the `build_inputs` definition (SD of the valid LR cells of the 256-px window, floored at 0.5 m) with the sha256 of `extract_clean_patches.py` at this commit; the tiles_v1.1 `s_lr` column equals `ref_s_lr` (asserted in R08).
11. **Working tree.** As in R07–R08b: uncommitted June 2026 edits remain in `models/`, `training/`, `preprocessing/data_utils.py`, `CLAUDE.md`, `METHOD.md`; `extract_clean_patches.py` (hashed into the pre-registration) and `colocated_utils.py` are not in the diff. COULD NOT CHECK beyond that.
