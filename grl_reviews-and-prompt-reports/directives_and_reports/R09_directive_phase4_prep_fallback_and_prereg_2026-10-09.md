## Part A — the interior-pixel fallback target (development units only)

### A1. Definition (`targets_v1_int`)

For each tile of `tiles_v1.1`:
- interior px = joint-valid px whose distance to the nearest invalid px is ≥ D₁ = ⌈lr_native / hr_res⌉ (the R08b
  distance transform, mosaic padded with invalid);
- **A_fine_int** = RMS of the frozen `F_hr` over the tile's interior px, computed only if the tile has **≥ 2,048
  interior px** (1/8 of a tile); otherwise NaN.

`F_hr` and `targets_v1.py` are unchanged; this is a new summary of the frozen field. Implement it as
`targets_v1_int.py` and record its sha256.

### A2. Validation on the development units

Score through `eval_v1_1` (within-pair Spearman ρ, pair eligibility ≥ 10 tiles) the existing **s_lr (ref_s_lr), GBT_ship
and P-V1 seed-averaged** predictions against A_fine_int. Report next to:
- (i) all-tile scoring against A_fine;
- (ii) R08b interior-tile scoring at D₁.

Report on the primary ten (lu14 shown separately), the nine without lu08, the six, and all 16.

Report:
- tiles with a defined A_fine_int, per pair and unit, against R08b's interior-tile counts (does lu14 gain tiles?);
- the paired median Δ (A_fine_int scoring − interior-tile scoring) per predictor, with bootstrap CI over units, units
  better and sign test.

**Validation rule (fixed now):** the fallback is **validated** if, on the primary units scored both ways, the absolute
paired median Δ is ≤ 0.05 for **both** s_lr and GBT_ship. Otherwise it is not validated. Report it as a HOLD, and Phase 4
uses interior tiles only.

### A3. Stress test at sealed-like cell sizes

Repeat A1–A2 on the development units with the interior distance computed for a **125 m** cell
(D₁₂₅ = ⌈125 / hr_res⌉ px; the target band is unchanged). Report, per unit:
- the interior tiles and the tiles with a defined A_fine_int;
- s_lr and GBT_ship scores against each target.

This shows how many tiles and units survive at sealed-like interior distances, and whether the ranking does.

### B4. Scoring rules

- **Statistic:** the `eval_v1_1` within-pair Spearman ρ on A_fine at tile scale. Each sealed pair is its own unit
  (nu01a, nu01b and nu03 are separate units under the leakage policy).
- **Tiles:**
  1. Interior tiles at D₁ (≥ 95 % of joint-valid px at ≥ D₁).
  2. If a pair has fewer than 10 interior tiles **and** the fallback was validated in R09 Part A, score it instead on
     tiles with a defined A_fine_int.
  3. If it still has fewer than 10, the pair is **not scorable** and is reported as such. There is no further fallback.
- **Uncertainty per pair:** a spatial-block bootstrap of ρ over 4 × 4-tile blocks, 2,000 resamples, seed 20261009, giving
  a 95 % percentile CI.
- **Secondary:** all-tile scores against A_fine, reported beside the primary.
- **Artifact handling:**
  - a pair judged **artifact-dominated** in B3.3 is reported separately and excluded from C1;
  - a pair with whole-survey stripes or ribbing is flagged, but stays in.

### B5. Criteria (fixed now)

- **C1, primary: ranking transfers.**
  - **Confirmed** if, on every scorable, non-artifact-dominated confirmatory unit, s_lr's ρ has a block-bootstrap CI
    lower bound > 0.
  - **Partially confirmed** if this holds on some but not all.
  - **Not confirmed** if on none.

  Expectation from development (interior tiles, primary units): unit ρ for s_lr between −0.09 and 0.63, median 0.39.
  Report each sealed unit's ρ against this range.
- **C2, secondary and descriptive: model complexity.** Per sealed unit, report:
  - GBT_ship − s_lr;
  - P-V1 − s_lr;
  - P-V1 − GBT_ship.

  Write one sentence per comparison stating its direction across units. No superiority claim is made: with at most 3
  units, even 3 of 3 gives a one-sided sign test of p = 0.125. State this in the result.
- **C3, supporting:** C1 and C2 on the lockbox pairs, reported separately with their history label.
