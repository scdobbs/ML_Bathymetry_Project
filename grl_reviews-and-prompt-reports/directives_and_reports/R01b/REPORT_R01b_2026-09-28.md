## 1. Bottom line

1. **H-norm: PARTIAL.** The border blow-ups occur on essentially every patch (97% of the worst-1% pixels lie
   within 8 px of the tile border, 0% near coverage holes, and the HR-mean fill step is not involved). Their
   extent is set by the border mechanism, not by relief or offset, for 90% of patches (about 2.5% of pixels
   with |μ| > 5 regardless of HR std). H-norm acts as an amplifier in the top decile: patches with
   max |input| > 5 or |normalized offset| > 2.2 have 25% of pixels with |μ| > 5, ten times the baseline, and
   that decile contributes about half of all blow-up pixels.
2. **Rim recommendation for R02: 40 px** (stride 176). Basis: the mean error profile reaches within 20% of
   its interior level at 28 px pooled and at 8 to 40 px per unit (AT37-13 40, TN399 33, EW0207 32). The
   directive's 99%-of-gradient rule cannot be applied: GroupNorm couples every output pixel to the whole
   tile, so the 99% radius is 165 px on a 256-px tile (see §2.1 and HOLD item 1).
3. **Loss recommendation for R02: standard Gaussian NLL.** The mean-abandonment signature (partial Spearman
   of |error| on σ controlling for input magnitude and border distance) is 0.24 in the rim and 0.09 in the
   interior (per-unit interior 0.07 to 0.15). The rim signal is the border artifact; the interior signal is
   not material. σ sits at its upper clamp on 2.9% of pixels, almost all in the rim.
4. **Leak inheritance.** §3.1 (support A) is clean: meters, ship features from the ship grid alone. §3.2
   (support B), the roughness heuristics and the metadata baseline all inherit the AUV normalization (scale
   via HR std; the metadata "depth" is the AUV patch mean and "fraction valid" uses the AUV mask). R02 must
   recompute all three baselines. With EW0207 excluded, the fine-band transfer in meters at N = 14 has
   pooled R² 0.74 and per-fold raw R² mean +0.25, median +0.49; Spearman positive on 14 of 14.
5. **Manifest.** `vert_offset_m` mixes two meanings: for six original-pipeline pairs it is the residual
   datum offset still present in the harmonized grids (correct for five; stale for AT37-13, repaired since);
   for the three "C1 float recovery" pairs it is the shift that was applied to the HR grid, so their grids
   now carry no offset. The SI sentence "vertical datum offsets were retained as diagnostic" is true for
   5 of 9 native pairs. Corrected values are proposed in §4; nothing was written.

### 2.1 B2 — Effective receptive field (gradient probing, 20 patches, 8 units)

Gradient of the learned correction μ − x (and of log σ²) at the tile center with respect to the input.

| quantity | radius holding 95% of gradient mass, median (max) | radius holding 99% | mass within 8 px | 16 px | 32 px | 64 px | mass beyond 64 px |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| μ − x | 139 px (163) | 165 px (174) | 0.46 | 0.59 | 0.71 | 0.77 | 0.23 (max 0.38) |
| log σ² | 146 px (166) | 165 px (174) | 0.29 | 0.44 | 0.63 | 0.73 | 0.27 (max 0.41) |

Reading (CONFIRMED). The theoretical convolutional field is 119 px (R01a B1), but the measured footprint
is tile-wide: 23 to 27% of the gradient mass lies beyond 64 px, spread over the whole tile. The cause is
`GroupNorm` in every block (`het_louo.py:52–56, 74–75`), whose per-tile statistics make every output
depend on every input pixel. Consequences: (i) a rim cannot isolate the border effect, because a window's
central pixels still see the padded border through the normalization statistics; (ii) the "99% radius"
rule in the directive returns 165 px, larger than a half tile, so no stride is possible under it; (iii) the
network's per-pixel outputs carry tile-level context, which in the leaked pipeline includes the HR-derived
normalization of the whole tile. The recommendation in §1 uses the empirical border decay (B4) instead.
Whether R02 keeps GroupNorm is a HOLD item (§6).

### 2.2 B3 — Blow-up anatomy against inputs (test of H-norm)

Worst 1% of |HR − μ| pixels versus all valid pixels, medians over 1,763 patches (1,563 excluding EW0207;
values identical to two decimals unless shown):

| local |normalized input| | 1.37 | 0.73 |
| lies within the LR-fill step (≤ 1 LR cell from an invalid LR cell) | 0.029 | 0.031 |

Per patch: fraction of pixels with |μ| > 5 has median 0.023, mean 0.046. Its Spearman correlation across
patches with HR std is 0.11, with max |input| 0.08, with |normalized offset| −0.12: at the patch level the
blow-up extent is nearly independent of the H-norm factors. The deciles show where H-norm does act:

| decile of | lowest decile | deciles 2 to 9 | top decile |
|---|---|---|---|
| HR std (m) | [0.13, 1.8] m: **0.158** | 0.022 to 0.060 | [33, 79] m: 0.037 |
| max |normalized input| | [0.56, 1.5]: 0.016 | 0.016 to 0.030 | [5, 150]: **0.249** |
| |normalized offset| | [0.0002, 0.045]: 0.029 | 0.014 to 0.032 | [2.2, 52]: **0.247** |

(entries are the mean fraction of |μ| > 5 pixels per patch.)

**Verdict: H-norm PARTIAL.** Two mechanisms are present. (a) A border mechanism on every patch: zero
padding at the tile edge produces |μ| > 5 on about 2.5% of pixels regardless of relief, offset or input
magnitude, and it accounts for roughly half of all blow-up pixels (deciles 1 to 9 of max |input|
contribute 0.021 of the 0.046 mean). (b) The H-norm amplifier: in the top decile of max |input| (> 5) or
|normalized offset| (> 2.2), which is the low-relief decile (HR std < 1.8 m), the blow-up covers 25% of the
tile and contributes the other half (0.249 × 0.1 ≈ 0.025 of 0.046). The HR-mean fill at LR coverage edges
(`P:312`) is CONTRADICTED as a cause: worst pixels are no more likely to lie in the fill step than any
pixel (0.029 vs 0.031). Per unit, the border share of worst pixels is 0.93 to 1.00 at NA090, NA080,
RR1506, FK181031, TN299 and 0.44 to 0.53 at AT37-13 and TN399, whose interiors are noisier.

### 2.4 B5 — σ behaviour and the mean-abandonment test

- σ clamp: 2.9% of pixels sit at the upper clamp (σ = 20.09; per unit 1.4% to 3.8%); 0% at the lower clamp.
  The rim population (about 2.5% of pixels with |μ| > 5) and the clamp population coincide.
- σ versus border distance: table above.
- Signature test, per patch, Spearman of |HR − μ| on σ after removing the rank-linear dependence of both on
  |normalized input| and border distance:

| region | partial ρ, median | plain Spearman, median | per-unit partial ρ range |
|---|---|---|---|
| rim (≤ 32 px) | 0.24 | 0.30 | 0.15 to 0.37 |
| interior (> 64 px) | 0.09 | 0.12 | 0.07 to 0.15 |

Reading. In the interior, σ carries little pixel-level information about the mean's error beyond what
input magnitude explains (0.09). The rim value (0.24) is the border artifact: μ blows up and σ inflates
with it, which is the NLL mean-abandonment pattern, but it is driven by zero padding, not by the loss
alone. **Recommendation: standard Gaussian NLL for R02.** β-NLL would be justified by a materially positive
interior partial correlation; 0.09 is not material. Steve decides.
