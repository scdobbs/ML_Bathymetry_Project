## Part B: a development check at the sealed geometry

The confirmatory 256-px windows will hold far fewer ship cells than any development window. From the ACQ-R02 records
(HR spacing 1.0, 1.037 and 1.0 m; ship grid cells 103.23, 125.15 and 150.74 m), about 2.5, 2.1 and 1.7 cells across,
against a development minimum of 3.88. This check measures, on development data, what s_lr does when its window holds
that few cells. It only adds an expectation line to v2. It changes no rule and no candidate.

1. **Records only.** Compute and report `cells_across = 256 × hr_res / cell` for the three confirmatory pairs from the
   ACQ-R02 records named above. State that the Phase 4 value uses `lr_native` from the supplement and may differ.
2. **Coarsened ship grid.** For each pair of the ten real-relief units:
   - block-average its `lr.tif` (valid cells only) by the integer factor f = round(125 / lr_native), with f ≥ 2, so
     that a cell is about 125 m;
   - recompute s_lr on each tile's 256-px window from the coarsened grid, with the frozen `build_inputs` definition
     unchanged (SD of the valid coarsened cells, floored at 0.5 m);
   - require at least 2 valid coarsened cells in the window, otherwise NaN.

   This uses ship data only. Nothing is derived from the AUV grid.
3. **Score** the coarsened s_lr against the frozen A_fine on interior tiles at D₁, through `eval_v1_1`, each pair as its
   own unit, with the B4 block bootstrap and B_min = 8. Report per pair, next to the original s_lr:
   - ρ and CI;
   - whether the CI lower bound is above zero;
   - cells across per window, and the fraction of tiles defined.

   Also report the C1 pass count over the C1-set pairs of the ten, and the pooled row with its CI (the R10b
   `pooled_row`, imported unchanged).
4. **One sentence of reading.** The fine-band target is not recomputed for the coarser cell. This check measures the
   predictor at sealed-like coarseness only, and the report must say so.
