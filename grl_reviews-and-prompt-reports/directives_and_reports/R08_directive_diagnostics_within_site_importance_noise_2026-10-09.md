## 1. Within-site upper reference

**Model.** A multi-feature GBT fit **within each pair**: the frozen feature set of GBT_ship (all features available for
that pair) and the log₁₀ A_fine target at tile scale. A single-feature reference is not used, because a monotone refit of
one feature cannot change Spearman ρ.

**Fixed configuration** (no tuning): `HistGradientBoostingRegressor(max_depth=3, max_iter=300, learning_rate=0.03,
min_samples_leaf=20, random_state=20261009)`.

**Cross-validation within the pair:**
- Partition the pair's tiles into **spatial blocks of 4 × 4 tiles** (in tile-grid coordinates from `tiles_v1_1`).
- Assign blocks to **5 folds** with `numpy.random.default_rng(20261009)`.
- **Buffer:** for each fold, remove from training every tile whose tile-grid position is within **2 tiles** (Chebyshev
  distance) of any test tile. This covers the 224-px feature windows, which overlap neighbouring tiles.
- Out-of-fold predictions for every tile of the pair.

**Feasibility rule (fixed now):** a pair is scored only if it has ≥ 80 tiles and ≥ 5 spatial blocks with at least one
test tile each, and every fold keeps ≥ 40 training tiles after the buffer. List infeasible pairs and units.

**Scoring:**
- Score the out-of-fold predictions with `eval_v1_1` (within-pair ρ, unit = median over the unit's feasible eligible
  pairs, median over feasible units).
- On the same feasible pairs and tiles, report the held-out GBT_ship (R05), s_lr and the best R06/R07 CNN arm
  (seed-averaged V2) for comparison.
- Report per unit and for the two groups.

**Table:** unit, feasible pairs, tiles used, within-site ρ, held-out GBT_ship ρ, s_lr ρ, V2 ρ, and within-site minus
held-out GBT_ship.

Assign every feature in the frozen feature table to exactly one group. List the mapping. Any feature not covered goes to
a group named "other", listed.

**Permutation importance (on held-out predictions):** for each held-out unit and each group, permute the group's columns
**jointly across the tiles of each pair** (one permutation of row order applied to all of the group's columns), predict

`numpy.random.default_rng(20261009)`. Importance = baseline unit ρ − mean permuted unit ρ.

**Drop-group refit (edge and validity group only):** refit GBT_ship with nested LOUO, removing dist_edge and frac_valid,
with the frozen grid and inner criterion. Score it.

**Report:**

- the median importance per group over the six and over the ten;
- the drop-group refit's ρ per unit and its difference from GBT_ship, for the six, the ten and all 16.

## 3. Fine-field inspection, all 16 units plus EW0207

### 3.1 Criteria (fixed now, before any figure is made)

A pair's fine-band field is judged **artifact-dominated** if its dominant pattern is any of:

- **(a)** stripes or banding parallel to AUV survey lines, or changes in texture at line boundaries;
- **(b)** a regular lattice or ribbing at a fixed spacing near the HR grid spacing or the AUV beam spacing;
- **(c)** concentration at data edges and holes, with the interior near uniform.

Otherwise the pair is judged **relief-dominated**. A pair can be "mixed", with the affected fraction of area stated.
Judgement is Steve's, made on the figures; this directive produces the figures and the objective companions.

### 3.2 Objective companions (per pair)

1. **Directional concentration:** the 2-D power spectrum of `F_hr` on each tile, averaged per pair. Report the fraction
   of fine-band power in the most energetic 10° sector of orientation, against the 10° expected for isotropy (0.056).
2. **Edge ratio:** RMS of `F_hr` over joint-valid px within 3 px of an invalid px, divided by RMS over px farther than
   10 px from any invalid px.
3. **Amplitude:** median A_fine (m) and its 10th–90th percentiles.

### 3.3 Figures

**Set A, blind** (for the judgement):
- For each pair, a two-panel figure: hillshade of HR (no labels), and the `F_hr` map with a symmetric colour scale at
  ±3 × its RMS.
- Same physical scale bar on every figure. No unit names, no scores, no amplitudes.
- Pairs in an order shuffled with `numpy.random.default_rng(20261010)`, coded `P01`, `P02`, … The key is written to a
  separate file, `figset_A_key.json`, which is not shown in the report.
- For pairs larger than about 2 km across, show a representative 2 km × 2 km subwindow at the centre of the
  joint-valid area, chosen by a fixed rule (the largest square inside the valid area, centred on its centroid).

**Set B, labelled:**
- The same panels with names, plus the tile A_fine map and the GBT_ship prediction map.
- The §3.2 metrics in the caption.

## 4. Site level across units

- median s_lr (m) over the same tiles;

**Report:**

  level);

## 6. Wide-window reference rows (no fitting)

**Inputs:** the ACQ-R05 context grids (`context_v1.parquet`, sha256 above; verify each pair's `ctx_lr.tif` against its
`SHA256SUMS`). For every `tiles_v1.1` tile, take the **64 × 64-cell window** of `ctx_lr.tif` centred on the tile
centre. Cells outside the context grid are invalid. Windows with < 50 % valid cells give NaN.

**Reference rows**, each computed with the **frozen function from `features_v1.py`**, unchanged, applied to the 64-cell
window in place of the 128-px ship window:
- **s_lr_W64:** the frozen s_lr definition (SD of valid cells, floored at 0.5 m);
- **C_W64:** roughness C on the ×8 bicubic, as frozen;
- **ŝ_fine_W64:** the frozen spectral extrapolation `spectral_extrap` (ŝ_fine), the zero-parameter prediction of
  fine-band amplitude from the power-law fit of the ship spectrum;
- **β_W64:** the frozen spectral slope, as a reported row.

State the fraction of tiles on which each row is defined, per unit.

**Scoring:** A_fine at tile scale under `eval_v1_1`, for each row, per unit and as the median with bootstrap CI over the
16 units, and again over the 15 without lu08. Report next to the frozen 128-px rows (s_lr, C), GBT_ship, and V2
seed-averaged. Give the differences (W64 row − the same row at 128 px) with CI, units better and sign test.

**Leak test:** recompute 100 tiles with the HR grid replaced by noise; the four rows must be byte-identical.
