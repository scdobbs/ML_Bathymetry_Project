## 2. Challenge clause (§0)

1. **§3 reading rule: the tie-break does not resolve V0 vs V1.** V0 and V1 differ only in the loss; they have the same inputs and the same window. Under the rule both are "simplest" among the variants within the seed spread of the best. `score_r06.py` breaks ties alphabetically and prints V0 with a tie note; that ordering is not a ruling. → HOLD H1.
2. **§2 V3: "cropped to the central 128 px; the head is unchanged" does not say which window the normalization and the FiLM statistics come from.** Implemented literally: the 256-px input built under §1.2 is cropped after normalization, so x is still (bicubic − mu_lr) / s_lr with the 256-window mu_lr and s_lr, and the FiLM vector is unchanged. → HOLD H2.
3. **Unspecified details, implemented as follows (executor's calls):**
   - Ship-product normalization: ship_rsd → log₁₀ max(rsd, 1 mm); ship_count → log₁₀ count; ship_xtrack_frac as is; ship_beam_angle / 90. Each product is area-averaged to the window's native-LR cells, unavailable cells are filled with the mean of the available cells, then bicubic to HR px; its availability mask is nearest-upsampled. Products of non-usable pairs and the overridden EX1402L2 xtrack are zero with a zero mask.
   - V2 fusion "before the head": a separate 3-level encoder (16, 32, 64 channels, FiLM-conditioned) on the 4 products and 4 masks; its output is cropped to the central tile, pooled to 4 × 4, and concatenated with the pooled decoder features before the head. Channel dropout zeroes a product's value and its mask together.
   - Head: crop the decoder output to the central 128 px, average-pool to 4 × 4, then a 1 × 1 conv (C → C), SiLU, 1 × 1 conv (C → 1), last layer zero-initialised (1,089 parameters).
   - V1 seed-averaged prediction: the mean of the three seeds' log₁₀ block predictions; the tile value is the RMS of those blocks.
4. **§1.5 schedule conversion.** The R02 recipe is 8,000 steps at batch 32; the §1.4 batch (4 groups × 8 windows) is also 32, so the step count carries over unchanged. No conversion was needed.
5. **Hardware.** The array ran on H100, A100 (40 and 80 GB) and L40S nodes; which fold landed on which GPU was not controlled. Each training is seeded and deterministic on its own GPU, but the same seed may not give bit-identical weights on another GPU type, so the measured seed spread also contains any hardware effect.
