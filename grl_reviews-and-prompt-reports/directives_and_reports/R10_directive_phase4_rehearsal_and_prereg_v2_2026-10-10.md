### B4, replaced

- **Statistic:** the `eval_v1_1` within-pair Spearman ρ at tile scale. Each sealed pair is its own unit.
- **Blocks:** 4 × 4-tile blocks in tile-grid coordinates (r0 / 128, c0 / 128). A block is non-empty if it holds at
  least one scored tile. **B_min = [value from R10 §A6.3].**
- **Tiles:**
  1. **Primary view:** interior tiles at D₁ (≥ 95 % of joint-valid px at ≥ D₁ = ⌈lr_native / hr_res⌉ px from any
     invalid px), scored against A_fine. Used if the pair has ≥ 10 interior tiles in ≥ B_min non-empty blocks.
  2. **Fallback view:** tiles with a defined A_fine_int (≥ 2,048 interior px), scored against A_fine_int. Used if rule 1
     fails and the pair has ≥ 10 such tiles in ≥ B_min non-empty blocks.
  3. Otherwise the pair is **not scorable** and is outside C1. If either view has ≥ 10 tiles, its point ρ is reported,
     labelled "too few blocks, not in C1", with no CI.
- **Uncertainty per pair:** a bootstrap over non-empty blocks, resampled with replacement to the same number of blocks,
  2,000 resamples, a fresh `numpy.random.default_rng(20261009)` per pair, 95 % percentile CI. Resamples with an
  undefined ρ are counted and excluded; if they exceed 5 %, the CI is reported as not estimable and the pair is outside
  C1.
- **Amplitude statistic:** the median of the scored target over the scored tiles of the view in use (m). It is computed
  at B3 step 2, before any prediction.
- **Secondary rows, for every pair with ≥ 10 tiles on that view:** all-tile ρ against A_fine; A_fine_int ρ where the
  primary view is in use. They are reported beside the primary and do not enter C1.
- **Artifact handling:**
  - **artifact-dominated** (`dominant = artifact`): reported separately and outside C1;
  - **low-amplitude artifact-affected** (`ab_whole_survey = yes` and amplitude statistic < 0.04 m): outside C1, and
    included in the second statement of C1;
  - any other pair with criteria a, b or c is flagged and stays in.

  The 0.04 m floor lies between the development groups: under interior scoring at D₁ the six artifact-affected units
  have unit medians of at most 0.029 m and the ten real-relief units at least 0.049 m (REPORT_R08b §5).
