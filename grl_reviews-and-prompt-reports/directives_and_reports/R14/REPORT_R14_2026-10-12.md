## 2. Challenge clause (§0) — wrong or underspecified instructions, with evidence, and the executor's calls

1. **§2.4 / §4.3 "fold models with the frozen configuration (cfg 18)" and "refit … exactly as the frozen bar was fit … reproduces … within 0.01".** The frozen bar (`bar_v1_1.nested_louo`) chose a configuration per outer fold by the inner criterion (`R05/bar/chosen_configs_tile.json`: 16 folds, 11 distinct configurations, none cfg 18); cfg 18 is the R09 all-16 artifact's configuration chosen by a 16-unit inner loop. No fold model was persisted. Both variants were refit and checked (§5.2); the variant that meets the reproduction bar is used for the `GBT_ship` column and both are reported → HOLD H1. Nothing was tuned.
2. **§3.3 "reproduces the frozen tiles, s_lr and features exactly".** s_lr exactly; features within 4.8 × 10⁻⁷ on `logcount_med_128/224` and ≤ 8 × 10⁻⁹ on `aniso_hr_128`, NaN pattern identical — the known float32 ulp between CPU generations (R10 HOLD H3; R10b ruling: 1e-6 absolute). Reported as CONFIRMED at the accepted tolerance.
3. **§5.2 and the interior distance map.** The frozen interior flag (R09 `targets_v1_int`, `phase4_build`) takes its distance map from `joint_v1.tif & isfinite(F_hr.tif)` over the FULL tiles_v1 mosaic, which also holds the crops of the tiles the v1.1 selection dropped. A first R14 run built the mosaic from the v1.1 tiles only (job 47583921: interior flags differed from R09); corrected to the frozen fields (OAK-verified) and rerun (47584757); the native level then reproduces the R09 flags on every tile. The joint mask, tile set, HR grid and native ship side of the target are frozen in the secondary analysis; only the band's lower edge and D₁ change, as §5.2 lists.
4. **§4.2 products "block averaged the same way".** Applied literally to all five contract products including `ship_count` (an average, not a sum, of sounding counts); `features_v1.pair_context`'s grid assertion (products grid == lr.tif grid) holds on the coarsened grids, so no masking was needed except tag_m127 (not usable at native).
5. **§1.3 "degrades materially" has no number.** The §1.1 yardstick (pooled drop ≥ 0.20 from the native level, or CI lower bound ≤ 0) is applied to GBT_ship as well in `tables/expectations.md`; the raw drops are given so that any other yardstick can be read off.
6. **"cells across = 256 × hr_res / lr_native".** hr_res is the harmonized HR raster spacing carried by tiles_v1.1 (as R10 §6.6 and `phase4_score` use it; e.g. 1.000138 m for RR1506, 2.054 m for KM0923), not the manifest's `hr_native_res_m` (0.992 m for RR1506); lr_native is the manifest's `lr_native_res_m` (the frozen path uses it for `lr_px` and D₁; the raster posting differs slightly, e.g. TN399__MGDS_30373 45.8 vs 44.18 m). The skip rule `lr_target ≤ lr_native` uses the manifest value; the coarse grid's cell is exactly lr_target, so lr_native' and posting coincide at every level.
7. **§5.3 "pooled mean over pairs".** Over the pairs with a scorable view at the level (as R11 Part B did), with the frozen native pooled row recomputed over the same pairs beside it (`pooled_frozen_same_pairs`), since the pair set changes with the level (5 pairs at 8.0, 13 at 4.0, fewer in the secondary analysis).
8. **Working tree.** As in R07–R13: uncommitted June 2026 edits remain outside the directives tree.
