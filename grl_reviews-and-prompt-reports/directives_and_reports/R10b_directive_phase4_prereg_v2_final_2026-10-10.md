## Part A: script changes

### A1. Pooled row (`phase4_score.py`)

Add one function, used by both modes:

- **Input:** a set of scored units (each sealed pair is its own unit), with s_lr and the scored target on the view in
  use, and each unit's non-empty blocks as defined in B4.
- **Statistic:** the mean over the units of s_lr's within-pair ρ.
- **CI:** one `numpy.random.default_rng(20261013)` per statement. Take the units in ascending pair-id order. For each
  unit in turn, draw 2,000 resamples of its non-empty blocks, with replacement, to the same number of blocks, and
  compute ρ on each. Resample r of the pooled statistic is the mean over units of their resample-r ρ. The CI is the
  95 % percentile interval.
- **Undefined resamples:** a resample in which any unit's ρ is undefined is dropped and counted. If more than 5 % are
  dropped, the CI is reported as not estimable.
- **One unit:** the row is the unit's own ρ and its CI under this generator. **No unit:** the row is not reported.

At Phase 4 the row is computed three times, each with a fresh generator:
1. on the C1 set;
2. on the C1 set plus the low-amplitude artifact-affected units;
3. on the scorable lockbox pairs (C3), reported separately.
